## What's happening in Olhão

Olhão was always the Algarve's most honest town — a working fishing port with the region's best market halls and zero interest in tourism. That's exactly why it's now the east's most interesting evening: the people priced out of Lisbon and charmed out of Tavira have been opening small wine bars, natural-wine spots and café-bars in the cubist backstreets, and the town has kept its rough edges while gaining places genuinely worth drinking in.

This is not a cocktail destination yet — it's a wine-and-petiscos town. The play is a slow evening: market-side terrace at golden hour, then into the backstreets for a second and third stop, dinner somewhere that smokes its own fish, done by midnight.

## Where to drink: the two zones

### 1. The waterfront (Avenida 5 de Outubro)

The twin red-brick **market halls** are Olhão's landmark, and the gardens and terraces around them face the Ria Formosa straight into the evening light. Any busy terrace here works for the first drink — you're paying local prices for a view the west coast would triple.

### 2. The cubist backstreets

Behind the waterfront, the old town's flat-roofed, white-cube lanes (the barrio that gave Olhão its Moorish nickname) hide the newer generation: wine bars pouring Portuguese small producers, café-bars with records on the wall, the odd spot doing simple cocktails well. This is where the town's changing fastest — wander, follow the noise.

## Olhão vs Tavira

**[Tavira](https://www.artonbar.com/tavira/)** is the polished east-Algarve evening — postcard square, established cocktail bars like Arcada. **Olhão** is the interesting one — rawer, more local, better food, faster-moving. They're 20 minutes apart; the correct answer is both, and if you're basing yourself in one for the nightlife trajectory, Olhão is the buy-low pick.

## Getting there

Olhão is 10km east of Faro — 15 minutes by car or a cheap, frequent train. That makes the killer combination easy: sunset at [LAB Terrace in Faro](https://www.artonbar.com/faro/lab-terrace/), train to Olhão for dinner and backstreet wine. No car needed.

## FAQ — Olhão Bars & Nightlife

Is Olhão good for a night out?

For dinner and drinks, increasingly yes — the east's trendiest scene. It's an evening town though: things wind down around midnight.

Where do you drink in Olhão?

Waterfront terraces by the market halls at golden hour, then the wine bars in the cubist backstreets behind them.

Olhão or Tavira?

Tavira for polish and established cocktail bars; Olhão for the newer, more local, faster-changing scene. 20 minutes apart — do both.

Open in winter?

Mostly — Olhão is a real working town with a year-round population. No resort hibernation.

How far from Faro?

10km — 15 minutes by car or a cheap frequent train. Sunset in Faro, dinner in Olhão is an easy no-car evening.

## Things to do in Olhão

Ria Formosa boat trips to the barrier islands (Armona, Culatra) leave from the Olhão waterfront.
