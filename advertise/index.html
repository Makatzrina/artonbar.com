Who reads ArtOnBar

People planning a night out in the Algarve — mostly British, Irish, German, Dutch and Portuguese visitors searching Google and asking AI assistants "best bars in Lagos", "rooftop bars Albufeira", "where to drink in Tavira". They arrive with a date and a budget. They're the customers you want on a Thursday.

The site covers 19 towns from Aljezur to the Spanish border, with town guides, vibe guides (sunset, rooftop, speakeasy, wine, beach, late-night, winter) and an interactive finder and map. Every page is written for search engines and for the AI models that now answer travel questions — which is where a growing share of "where should we go tonight" decisions are made.
