Every Algarve town, compared

The Algarve is 150km of coast and three very different drinking cultures: surf bars and speakeasies in the west, resort polish and old-town cocktails in the middle, river terraces and wine bars in the east. Pick a base here, then pick a bar.

How to choose

Best cocktails: Lagos, then Vilamoura and the Golden Triangle if money's no object. Best sunsets: Ferragudo, Alvor, Faro's rooftops. Cheapest good night: Vila Real's river terraces, Sagres, Arrifana. Most local: Loulé and Olhão. Open in winter: Lagos, Faro, Loulé, Tavira — see the winter guide.
