No. Paying buys position: the top of a page, the homepage card or a banner, always labelled. The review is written after a real visit and doesn't change with payment.

People in the Algarve, or about to arrive, deciding where to drink. In the last 30 days: 57,000 visitors and 127,000 page views, up 19% on the month before, almost all from Google searches like "best bars in Lagos".

How fast does a featured listing go live?

Within 24 hours of payment, usually faster. You get the link by email.

Can I pay by bank transfer instead of card?

Yes. Send the enquiry, pick the package, and ask for an invoice. Card payment through Stripe is the fastest route.

My bar isn't on the site yet. Can I still advertise?

Yes. The review visit comes first and is free. If the bar is good, it goes on the site and the featured spot can follow. If it isn't, you'll hear it privately and we'll talk again later.
