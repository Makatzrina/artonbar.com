Bars in Aljezur & Arrifana: Surf Bars on the Wild Coast (2026)

There are no cocktail bars in Aljezur. The best bars on this coast are surf bars on the cliff above Praia da Arrifana, where the terrace fills at 7pm with people still in wetsuits, a beer is €2.50–3, a caipirinha €7, and the only event on the programme is the sun going into the Atlantic. This is the Costa Vicentina: a natural park, no neon, bars that close before midnight and a sunset that doesn't.

Arrifana is where the evening happens

Praia da Arrifana is a shell-shaped bay under high cliffs with a 12th-century castle ruin on the headland, and the handful of bars on the cliff top face dead west. Bar da Praia is down on the sand — fish, fresh juices and tapioca pancakes by day, beer at sunset. Sea You Surf Café is up above the bay with cheap drinks and a vegetarian-friendly kitchen. Sector B on the road in does burgers, beers and a diner room full of surfers. On summer weekends there's occasionally live music — a guitarist, never a DJ — and the bars stay open till midnight or so. Midweek and off-season you'll be one of six people, which has its own appeal.

Arrive by 7pm in summer for a seat. Bring a jumper; Atlantic evenings turn cold fast.

Aljezur village and the other beaches

In the village, Moagem — an old mill turned bar and vegetarian café — is the cultural end of Aljezur: natural wines, a terrace, an expat-and-local mix. The specialty-coffee places double as daytime hangouts. For food before the bar, Pont'a Pé is the traditional classic.

Monte Clérigo beach has a calmer, family-leaning sunset bar; Amoreira's bar sits where the river meets the sea and is a one-drink stop; Odeceixe, 20 minutes north, has its own beach bar and a village square with a couple of places. All seasonal, all cash-friendly.

Practicalities

A car is essential and drinking-and-driving on the unlit coast road is a terrible idea: stay near wherever you're drinking. Most beach bars run June to September only; the village bars and Sector B run longer. Cash is useful. And manage expectations: this is not nightlife territory. The sunset is the entertainment, and it's enough.

FAQ — Aljezur & Arrifana bars & nightlife

Is there nightlife in Aljezur?
No clubs, no cocktail bars. A handful of surf bars at Arrifana with sunset as the main event, and village bars that close before midnight. For a proper night out, Lagos is 45 minutes.
Where do you watch the sunset at Arrifana?
From the cliff-top bars above the beach or down on the sand at Bar da Praia. The bay faces west; arrive by 7pm in summer for a seat.
Are Arrifana bars open in winter?
Most beach bars close from October to May. Sector B, Sea You and the Aljezur village bars run year-round with reduced hours, serving the winter surf crowd.
How much is a drink in Arrifana?
Beer €2.50–3, caipirinha around €7, wine €3–4 a glass. Cheapest coast in the Algarve.
How far is Aljezur from Lagos and Sagres?
Lagos is 45 minutes by car, Sagres about 40. There's no useful public transport to the beaches.

Things to do in Aljezur & Arrifana

Surf lessons at Arrifana and Amado, Costa Vicentina hikes, Aljezur castle.
