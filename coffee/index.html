Specialty coffee in the Algarve,
town by town

Good coffee is one of my hobbies, the kind I like to nurture, and I'm always glad to find a place that does a “proper” cup. In the eastern Algarve that's a bit of a gap, but here's what I found. Where a town has one real specialty bar, I say so, and I list the best traditional pastelarias as the honest alternative rather than padding the page. Hours are checked against the café's own listing on the date shown; winter hours shrink, so look at their Instagram before you drive.

Proper means proper.

Fresh beans, a machine someone cleans, milk that's steamed rather than boiled. If a town has one place like that, the page says one.

Hours matter more than hype.

Half the good cafés in the east open at nine and close by two, or trade three days a week. Every listing shows when, and when it was checked.

Winter is coffee season.

The bars go quiet from November, the cafés don't. This section is for the people who live here, work here, or ride here when it's 17 degrees and sunny.
