## 6 places that make coffee properly

1

[Nomads Specialty Coffee ↗](https://nomadsandco.pt/coffee-corner)

Av. Tomás Cabreira, Ed. Varandas da Rocha 81, loja 26 · Daily 09:00–17:00, year-round · €3.2

An e-scooter rental with a serious coffee corner: Al-Gharb beans from Albufeira, flat white €3.20, oat or almond for 20 cents more, homemade cookies. A stand rather than a lounge, and one of the few things on the strip open all winter.

[Rocha Beach House ↗](https://rochabeachhouse.com)

Av. Tomás Cabreira, Ed. Rochamar, loja 5 · Mon–Sat 10:00–17:00, closed Sun

Surf shop, poké bar and a real coworking room with adjustable desks, fast internet and a phone booth, day passes available. Opens late, at ten.

[Wild's Sandwich Boutique ↗](https://wildsandwich.com)

Rua Quinta da Praia, lote 4, loja 8 · Wed–Sun 09:00–16:00, closed Mon–Tue

Irish-run sandwich and brunch bar between Alvor village and the beach, self-described specialty coffee, cinnamon buns and bagels cooked to order. 'Four dogs this morning', says a review approvingly.

4

[Velochique Vista Rio ↗](https://linktr.ee/velochique)

Largo do Dique 18, loja 2 (riverfront) · Mon & Wed–Sun 08:30–17:00, closed Tue

The riverfront sister of Monchique's cycling café, and the best coffee in Portimão proper: batch brew and espresso with latte art, lemon tart, bike parking out front.

[Coffeine & Brunch ↗](https://www.instagram.com/coffeine.and.brunch/)

Rua Caetano Feu 2 · Daily 09:00–16:00

American-style brunch a street back from the strip, gluten- and dairy-free pancakes, oat lattes (small ones), Wi-Fi and outlets. Good coffee by reviewers' accounts; no specialty sourcing stated.

[Clarice's ↗](https://claricesbrunchalvor.pt)

Japanese soufflé pancakes and cinnamon buns in Alvor village, closed until 5 January 2027. Reviews praise the food and grumble about rushed service.

## Traditional, and good at it

Not specialty, and not pretending to be. These open early, serve a decent bica and the pastries the town actually eats.

1

[Cult n' Art Café ↗](https://www.facebook.com/CultnArtCafe)

Rua do Castelo 18 · Wed–Sat 09:00–15:00, closed Sun–Tue · €1

The highest-rated café in Alvor: brunch, sourdough from the village bakery, kombucha, an art shop, €1 espresso. Not specialty-sourced, and nobody minds.

⭐ 4.9

2

Pastelaria Arade

Largo 1º de Dezembro 4 · Tue–Sat 08:00–21:00, Sun 08:00–18:00, closed Mon

One of the oldest pastelarias in Portimão: Dom Rodrigo, almond cake, natas, and a coffee reviewers keep calling amazing for what it is.

⭐ 4.5
