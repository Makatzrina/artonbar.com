Specialty coffee in Loulé & Almancil

In Loulé there are only two places with proper coffee: bean17 inside the market, open until about one in the afternoon, and Mellow, which has good coffee, good cakes, a nice room and cocktails later on. Everywhere else the coffee tastes burnt. In Almancil, The Showroom.

Visited Mellow Café
Rua Dom Paio Peres Correia 18 · Tue–Sat 09:30–15:30, closed Sun–Mon

One of only two places in Loulé where the coffee is made properly. A small, vintage-looking room with mismatched china, good homemade cakes by the slice, and cocktails later in the day. Took over the old Cinnaroma bakery premises.

Buganvilia Plaza 10 (towards Quinta do Lago) · Daily 08:00–15:30 · €2.9

Espresso, batch brew and açaí bowls at a small counter in an outdoor plaza near Quinta do Lago, under the bougainvillea. Open every day from eight, which nothing else around here is; recent complaints about brusque service and paper cups.
