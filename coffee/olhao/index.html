Specialty coffee in Olhão

Olhão has exactly one specialty coffee bar, and it's a good one. The rest is traditional, and some of that is excellent too, so the list is short and honest.

Hours checked 2026-11-19. Visited means we've had the coffee ourselves; the rest is from the cafés' own listings and recent reviews, with Google ratings where shown.

Café Tinto ↗
Rua Manuel Lopes de Almeida 3A · Mon & Thu–Sun 09:00–14:00, closed Tue–Wed · €3.9

Olhão's one specialty coffee bar, opened in 2024 by Mo and Talia, 5.0 on Google from hundreds of reviews: beans scored 85+, deli sandwiches, oat milk, dogs welcome. Tiny, a declared laptop-free zone, and shut by two.

Casa Amor ↗
Rua Dr. Pádua 24A · No published hours; call +351 910 669 436

The ground-floor café of a ten-room townhouse hotel run by Parisian owners, known in town for French pastries. A calm, pretty room; specialty credentials unverified, so it's here for the pastries.

The honest alternative

Traditional, and good at it

Not specialty, and not pretending to be. These open early, serve a decent bica and the pastries the town actually eats.
