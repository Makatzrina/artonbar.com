## 4 places that make coffee properly

1

[Evolution Brunch ↗](https://evolutionstore.eu)

Rua Eng. Aboim Sande de Lemos 1 · Thu–Mon 09:00–17:00, closed Tue–Wed

Spacious brunch room north of the centre, attached to an EV showroom, listed on Google as a coworking space and easy to work from. Great plates; nobody writes about the coffee itself, so we haven't ranked it on that.

[Melbourne Lane Cafe ↗](https://www.instagram.com/melbourne.lane.cafe/)

Rua Manuel Belmarço 6 · Daily 08:30–16:00

The specialty coffee and brunch most people in Faro rate first: Aussie-style, bagels and waffles, always busy. Free Wi-Fi, no stated laptop policy.

[Yellow Bourbon Roastery & Store ↗](https://yellowbourbon.pt)

Rua Baptista Lopes 26 · Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00, Sat 09:00–14:00, closed Sun · €3.5

The roastery itself, with an espresso bar: pick your bean (Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia, Guatemala), good decaf, bags to take home. Two flat whites about €7.

[Yellow Bourbon Specialty Coffee Bistro ↗](https://yellowbourbon.pt)

Rua Vasco da Gama 19 · Mon–Sat 09:00–18:00, Sun 09:00–15:00

The bistro in the yellow building near the old town, pouring beans roasted at its own roastery 150 metres away. Terrace, pastries, gluten-free and vegan options; the warm dishes are the weaker part.

## Traditional, and good at it

Not specialty, and not pretending to be. These open early, serve a decent bica and the pastries the town actually eats.

1

Pastelaria Padaria Centeio

Rua Mouzinho de Albuquerque 25 · Mon–Fri 07:00–20:00, Sat–Sun 07:00–13:00

Opens at seven. Fresh pastel de nata (€1.50), Faro's own Dom Rodrigo egg sweets and a galão at the counter. The seven-o'clock answer.

sun open opens 07:00

⭐ 4.5

2

[Pastelaria Coelho ↗](https://pastelariacoelho.shop)

Rua Brites de Almeida 2 · Sun–Fri 07:00–24:00, closed Sat

Pastelaria and cheap-lunch institution open from seven until midnight: bifana, nata, a decent cappuccino, slow when it's full.
