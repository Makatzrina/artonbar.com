Specialty coffee in Lagos

Lagos is the Algarve's coffee capital by a distance: roasters, laptop-friendly rooms, brunch queues. The problem isn't finding a flat white, it's choosing.

Hours checked 2026-11-19. Visited means we've had the coffee ourselves; the rest is from the cafés' own listings and recent reviews, with Google ratings where shown.

New in autumn 2026: fruity espresso, cold brew, natural wine in the evening. Fifteen reviews so far, all glowing. Promising, not yet proven.

Clifftop Coffee Roasters ↗
LACO Business Hub, Workshop 4, Chinicato · Tue 09:00–17:30 only (bean pick-up and cuppings)

A roastery, not a café: roasts weekly since 2021 and supplies cafés across the Algarve. Worth a line for residents buying beans.

DOT Coffee ↗
Rua Prof. Luís de Azevedo 2 · Wed–Sun 08:30–17:00, closed Mon–Tue

The highest-rated coffee in Lagos: a few tables, properly textured milk, vegan pastries, guest roasters from Rotterdam and Portugal. A sign asks you not to camp all day with a laptop.

The Studio (marina) ↗
Marina de Lagos, lote 31 F · Daily 07:30–15:30

The Studio's second Lagos site with a mini roastery you can watch, batch brew and a gluten-free almond-orange cake. Small, and the easiest Studio to reach without cobbles.

Everyday People Coffee ↗
Rua Conde Lippe · Daily 08:00–16:00 · €2.8

Marina side, outside the cobbles: a separate free coworking room, rotating European roasters, oat milk at no extra charge, cookies and sandwiches rather than pastries. The cyclists' and remote workers' pick.

Coffee & Waves ↗
Travessa do Cotovelo 10 · Tue–Sun 09:00–16:00; closed when the surf is good · €4.5

Lagos's original third-wave bar: a walk-up window, guest roasters from Koyo to Friedhats, bags for sale, açaí bowls. A few bar seats, pricey, and the least predictable hours in town.

Roasts its own beans in-house, opens at seven, and has a dedicated coworking bar with sockets. The most work-ready specialty café in the Algarve; no sign outside, so trust the address.

The busiest brunch in the old town, with a takeaway counter next door that skips the queue. Laptops only on the window stools, by design. Year-round and consistent.
