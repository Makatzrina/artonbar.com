# ArtOnBar — outreach email templates

Rules that work: short, show them their own page, one ask, no attachments. Send Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–noon, from your own address (not a no-reply). Follow up twice, then stop.
Find the contact: the bar's website "contact" page, the email in their Instagram bio, or walk in on a quiet afternoon and ask for the owner/manager — in-person works best for bars.

---

## 1. Cold pitch — bar that is already listed

**Subject:** Your bar is on ArtOnBar — quick question

Hi {first name / team},

I run ArtOnBar.com, the Algarve bar guide. {Bar name} is already on it — here's your page:
{page URL}

It's read by people searching Google and asking ChatGPT "best bars in {town}" before they go out. The site covers 19 towns and 96 bars; {town} readers see your listing {position} on the page.

I'm opening a small number of Featured Listings per town: your bar pinned to the top of the {town} page, the Bar Finder and the map, with a "Featured" tag and a direct link to your booking page or Instagram. €49 a month, cancel any time. The review itself stays honest — that's why people trust the page.

Would it be worth 10 minutes next week for me to show you the numbers for your page?

{Your name}
ArtOnBar.com · {phone / WhatsApp}

## 2. Cold pitch — bar not yet reviewed (sell the review)

**Subject:** Reviewing {bar name} for ArtOnBar

Hi {first name / team},

I write ArtOnBar.com, the Algarve's independent bar guide (19 towns, 96 bars, read by people planning a night out in {town}).

{Bar name} keeps coming up in my {town} research and I'd like to give it a full review page — its own URL, address and hours, what to order, an FAQ that Google and the AI assistants pick up. Here's what one looks like: https://www.artonbar.com/lagos/forbidden-door/

The package is €149 for the review and visit plus €49 a month to keep it Featured at the top of the {town} page. I pay for my own drinks and the verdict is mine — if something isn't working I'll tell you privately first.

Good time for me to come by? Weekday afternoons are easiest.

{Your name}
ArtOnBar.com · {phone / WhatsApp}

## 3. Event listing (quick, low-ticket, opens the door)

**Subject:** Your {live music / DJ / sunset party} on ArtOnBar's What's On

Hi {first name},

Saw your {event} on Instagram. I've just launched a What's On page for Algarve bars — https://www.artonbar.com/whats-on/ — and I'd like to list it.

It's €29 per event (or €29/month for a weekly night), linked to your booking page. Free if you take a Featured Listing. Reply with the date, time and a line about it and I'll put it up today.

{Your name}

## 4. Follow-up (5 days later)

**Subject:** Re: {original subject}

Hi {first name} — floating this back up. Your page is here: {page URL}. If now isn't the time, no problem; tell me who's best to speak to and I'll leave you alone.

{Your name}

## 5. Second follow-up (10 days after that) — last one

**Subject:** Last one from me

Hi {first name} — I'll stop here. The listing stays up free regardless. If you ever want the Featured spot in {town} before a competitor takes it, the page is https://www.artonbar.com/advertise/.

{Your name}

---

## 6. In-person script (30 seconds, quiet afternoon)

"Hi — I run ArtOnBar, the Algarve bar guide. You're already on it (show the page on your phone). I'm offering a handful of bars in {town} the top spot on the page for €49 a month. Who's the right person to talk to about that?"
Leave a card with the page URL and /advertise/ written on it.

---

## Objections you'll hear

- "We're full in summer anyway." → "Then sell the shoulder season. October to May is when the page gets the most 'open in winter' searches."
- "We get enough from Instagram." → "Instagram reaches people who already follow you. This reaches people who've just landed and typed 'best bars in {town}'."
- "Can I see traffic numbers?" → Share your GA4 page views for their town page and the affiliate_click events; be honest if it's early.
- "Can you change the rating?" → "No. That's the whole reason the page is worth being on."
