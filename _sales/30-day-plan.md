# 30-day plan to first revenue

## Before you send anything (day 1–2)
- [ ] Confirm the GetYourGuide partner ID and put it in js/config.js (so the numbers you show bars are real).
- [ ] Create Stripe Payment Links (Products → Payment links): €49/month recurring "Featured Listing", €149 one-off "Review", €99 "Sponsored post", €29 "Event". Paste the URLs into payment_links in js/config.js. /advertise/ buttons become "Buy now".
- [ ] Set up a free Google Business Profile for ArtOnBar? No — not needed. Do set up a WhatsApp Business number; bar owners reply on WhatsApp, not email.
- [ ] Open outreach-list.csv in Google Sheets. Fill contact_email for the priority-1 rows (website contact page or Instagram bio).

## Week 1 — priority 1 towns (Lagos, Vilamoura, Golden Triangle)
- [ ] Send template 1 to every listed bar with its own review page (Forbidden Door, Sal Rosa, The Argo, LAB Terrace, etc.). They already know you; these are the easiest yeses.
- [ ] Send template 2 to the five highest-rated unreviewed bars in Lagos (KasBah, Mar d'Estórias, Pepper's, Bon Vivant, Stevie Ray's).
- [ ] Walk the Vale do Lobo Praça one afternoon: The Roof, Beach Bar, The Wolf, Gecko — script 6. Resort bars have budgets and a manager on site.
- Target: 15 emails, 4 visits, 2 replies.

## Week 2 — priority 2 (Albufeira, Alvor, Carvoeiro, Praia da Luz) + follow-ups
- [ ] Template 4 to everyone from week 1 who didn't reply.
- [ ] Alvor is one street: walk it at 5pm, start at Sunset Bar. Offer the event tier for their Thursday live music as the opener.
- [ ] Template 3 to every bar with a recurring night (Sunset Bar Alvor, The Shack, Beach Bar VdL, Dano's, Stevie Ray's).
- Target: first paid event listing (€29) and first Featured (€49).

## Week 3 — priority 3 + the "first customer" push
- [ ] Faro, Tavira, Portimão, Loulé, Ferragudo via email; Edd's Beach and Guarita Terrace in person if you're east/west anyway.
- [ ] Switch the first paying bar on the same day (README: "Switching a Featured Listing on"). Screenshot it. That screenshot is your pitch for everyone else.
- [ ] Post the new Featured bar in the newsletter and on Instagram, tag them. Bars sell each other.

## Week 4 — second follow-ups, pricing check, repeat
- [ ] Template 5 to the silent ones. Mark status in the sheet; stop chasing.
- [ ] If more than 1 in 5 said yes: raise Featured to €69 for new signups. If fewer than 1 in 15 replied at all: the ask is too big — lead with the €29 event listing everywhere.
- [ ] Monthly ritual from here: 1st of month invoices; a one-line email to each Featured bar with their page views and clicks.

## Numbers to aim for
- 10 Featured bars = €490/month. 20 = €980/month. Cap at ~3 per town so "Featured" still means something.
- One review package a month = €149 extra + the recurring.
- Keep a public "Featured bars" count honest; scarcity is the sales argument.
