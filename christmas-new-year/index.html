Christmas & New Year's Eve in the Algarve: where to drink

The short version: the 24th closes early, the 25th closes almost entirely, the week between is as busy as June in the big resorts, and New Year's Eve is a free council party with fireworks in most towns. Here is what is confirmed for 2026, what last year looked like where it isn't, and which bars you can count on.

We update this page as the councils publish. Last year's events are labelled as last year's.

New Year's Eve 2026: what's confirmed

Three towns had published their programmes when we checked. All three are free council events in the open air.

  • Albufeira: Carpe Nox 2027. Calema play from 10pm on 31 December, building to a midnight show the mayor describes as more than 1,250 drones plus pyrotechnics, then DJ Nox. Carolina Deslandes plays the first sunset of 2027 on 1 January. Last year's edition was on Praia dos Pescadores and drew over 230,000 people across the weekend; the 2026 venue hasn't been stated officially but there's no reason to expect a change. Council announcement · Sul Informação. Drink before or after at Sal Rosa, five minutes up the hill; it will be rammed, book the front tables. Note Albufeira's new closing rules since June 2026: bars close by 3am, clubs by 5am.
  • Quarteira: Pedro Abrunhosa and fireworks at midnight on 31 December, Plutónio on 30 December, a 5/10 km fun run on the 29th. Venue is Jardim Filipe Jonas (not the usual seafront, because of market works). Free. Times not yet published. Programme. The Quarteira bars are a ten-minute walk; if you're staying in Vilamoura, this is your free party.
  • Lagos: Nininho Vaz Maia on Praça do Infante. The council confirmed the headliner in March; times, fireworks and the rest of the line-up are still to come. Last year it was Pedro Abrunhosa from 10:30pm with what the council called one of the longest fireworks displays in the Algarve, free entry. Council. Every bar on the Lagos page is within five minutes of the square.

Not yet announced: what last year looked like

These towns all ran free midnight fireworks for 2025/26 and had not published 2026 programmes by 18 November. Expect something similar; we'll update when they do.

  • Portimão / Praia da Rocha. Last year: concerts on the Portimão riverside (10pm), an 11-minute pyromusical on the Arade at midnight, plus fireworks from the Praia da Rocha jetty and from Alvor. Most strip bars are shut for winter by then; the year-round exceptions are the casino and marina bars, and Outro Bar above the beach. The AP Oriental hotel on the strip runs a €180 gala with its own midnight fireworks.
  • Faro. Last year: Jardim Manuel Bivar in the Baixa, Sara Correia, pyromusical at midnight, DJ after, free. The Faro rooftops are a five-minute walk from the garden; LAB Terrace closes at 8pm, so it's your pre-party, not your midnight.
  • Tavira. Last year: Praça da República from 10pm, Queen tribute band, pyromusical from the Ponte dos Descobrimentos at midnight, DJ until 4am. Arcada is on the square itself. Christmas lights and markets in the historic centre run 6 December to 6 January.
  • Olhão. Last year: Jardim Pescador Olhanense, Diogo Piçarra, fireworks over the Ria at midnight, free. The market-hall bars are right there. Feira de Natal runs 11 to 24 December 2026 on the waterfront.
  • Carvoeiro. Last year: on the beach from 10:30pm, pyromusical at midnight, DJs until 3am, free; Levels was a sponsor and is on the square above the sand.
  • Vilamoura marina. No free council event; Loulé's public party is Quarteira (above). The paid route is a hotel gala: Tivoli's Réveillon with Mariza from about €495, Hilton from €340, both with midnight fireworks. The Argo and the marina bars open as normal.
  • Sagres. No council concert programme last year either; a community party at the sports pavilion and municipal fireworks were budgeted. Dromedário and Pau de Pita are open in winter and are where the town ends up.

Christmas Eve and Christmas Day: the honest reality

  • 24 December: bars, restaurants and shops close around 6 to 7pm. Portuguese Christmas is the evening of the 24th. A late lunch drink, then that's it.
  • 25 December: the one day the Algarve closes. Supermarkets, malls, most restaurants. In Lagos, Albufeira, Praia da Rocha and Vilamoura a handful of bars open, often serving a full Christmas dinner, and those tables are booked out weeks ahead. If a Christmas Day drink matters to you, book it before you travel and stay in one of those four towns.
  • 26 December to 1 January: normal. From about the 21st the big resorts feel as busy as late June and most bars that are open in winter run extended hours. 1 January is a holiday again: slow start, bars open in the afternoon.
  • Getting home on the 31st: the last train out of Faro is 10:10pm. After that it's Bolt, taxi or a bed within walking distance.

FAQ

Are bars in the Algarve open on Christmas Day?
Some. The 25th is the one day the country closes: shops, supermarkets and most restaurants. In Lagos, Albufeira, Praia da Rocha and Vilamoura a handful of bars open, often with a Christmas lunch or dinner that sells out weeks ahead. Book before you fly. Smaller towns (Sagres, Carvoeiro, Tavira) are largely shut on the 25th.
What about Christmas Eve?
Almost everything, bars included, closes around 6–7pm on 24 December for family dinner. Plan an early drink or none at all. The 26th is a normal working day and bars reopen.
Where are the New Year's Eve fireworks in the Algarve?
Confirmed for 31 December 2026 so far: Albufeira (council show with Calema, from 10pm), Quarteira (Jardim Filipe Jonas, Pedro Abrunhosa, fireworks at midnight) and Lagos (Praça do Infante, Nininho Vaz Maia). Portimão, Faro, Tavira, Olhão and Carvoeiro all ran free midnight fireworks last year and had not published their 2026 programmes when we checked on 18 November.
Do I need a ticket for New Year's Eve?
The municipal events are free, no ticket. The hotel galas (Vilamoura, Praia da Rocha) are the paid option: from about €99 for a buffet dinner to €400+ for a gala with a headline act.
Is Vilamoura marina good for New Year's Eve?
There is no free council event at the marina; Loulé's public party is in Quarteira, ten minutes away. The marina bars open, and the hotels run paid galas with their own midnight fireworks.
