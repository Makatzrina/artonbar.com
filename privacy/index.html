Privacy policy

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and what you can ask me to delete.

Who runs this site

ArtOnBar.com is an independent bar guide to the Algarve, Portugal, run by a private individual. Contact: via the contact page.

What's collected

  • Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read. Google sets cookies for this and processes the data on its servers; IP addresses are anonymised by GA4. See Google's privacy policy. You can block it with a browser extension such as the GA opt-out add-on.
  • Forms. The contact, newsletter and "list your bar" forms are delivered by FormSubmit.co to the site's email address. What you type is used only to reply to you or send the newsletter. Unsubscribe with one click on any newsletter.
  • Advertising. If display ads are shown, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your browsing. Opt out at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Affiliate links. Clicking a Booking.com or GetYourGuide link takes you to their site, where their own cookies and privacy policies apply. They may record that the visit came from ArtOnBar so a commission can be paid.
  • Embedded content. Some review pages embed Instagram posts and GetYourGuide widgets, and the map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Those services may set their own cookies.

What's not collected

No accounts, no passwords, no payment details, no selling of email addresses. Ever.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask what data I hold about you (realistically: an email address if you wrote to me) and ask for it to be deleted. Email me and I'll do it within a week.

Cookies

Cookies on this site come from Google Analytics, Google AdSense (if ads are shown) and embedded third-party content. The site itself sets none. Your browser settings let you block or delete them.
