Bars in Ferragudo: Sunset Beach Clubs & a Village Square (2026)

Ferragudo is the fishing village you can see from Portimão's marina — white houses stacked up the hill, a square of café terraces, and two beaches tucked behind the headland. The best bars in Ferragudo are on those beaches: Edd's Beach, a west-facing club where the sun actually sets into the ocean, and the beach bar and rooftop at Praia dos Caneiros.

Why Edd's Beach is on every list

Most Algarve beaches face south, which means sunset happens somewhere behind the cliffs. Praia do Pintadinho faces west. Edd's Beach sits above it with sunbeds, a cocktail bar, Neapolitan pizza and wine from the owners' own vineyard, and from about an hour before sunset the whole terrace turns to face the same direction. It's open all year, six days a week (closed Tuesdays), with a €30-per-person food minimum on busy days. Book.

It is not cheap and it is not a secret. It's still the best sunset drink between Lagos and Carvoeiro.

Caneiros and the village

Praia dos Caneiros, the next cove east, has Rei das Praias: a beach bar from 10am to 11pm in season, a restaurant and a rooftop terrace from 12:30pm with cocktails looking straight at the cliffs. Less polished than Edd's, more Portuguese, more families.

Back in the village, the café-bars on the main square do the honest thing: €2 beers, a glass of wine, a view across the Arade to Portimão's lights. There's no cocktail bar in Ferragudo proper worth a detour — for that, Praia da Rocha is across the bridge.

FAQ — Ferragudo bars & nightlife

Is Ferragudo good for a night out?
For a sunset drink and dinner, excellent. For bars after midnight, no — the village winds down early. Portimão and Praia da Rocha are 10 minutes across the bridge.
Do I need to book Edd's Beach?
Yes, especially for sunset in summer and any weekend. Reservations by phone or Instagram; closed Tuesdays.
Is Edd's Beach open in winter?
Yes — it's one of the few beach clubs on the coast that stays open all year, six days a week.
How do I get to Pintadinho and Caneiros?
Both are a 5-minute drive or a 25-minute walk from Ferragudo village along the road past the fort. Parking is tight in August.
Ferragudo or Carvoeiro?
Ferragudo for the sunset beach club and village calm; Carvoeiro, 15 minutes east, for a bigger choice of cocktail bars and a livelier square.

Things to do in Ferragudo

Benagil cave boat trips leave from Ferragudo and Portimão marina — do the caves in the afternoon, Edd's at sunset.
