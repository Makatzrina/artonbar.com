# Algarve Bars Open in Winter — 2026

Half the Algarve bar scene locks up from October. The other half is often better in winter — quieter, more local, no queues, and run by people who actually want to be there. These are the bars that stay open when the tourists leave, and the towns worth visiting for a winter drink.

## What to avoid in winter

Sky Bar Carvoeiro closes seasonally — don't make a trip for it outside April to October. Most of the Praia da Rocha cliff-top strip on Av. Tomás Cabreira is shut from mid-October to late May (Outro Bar included); the casino bar, the marina bars and Alvor's pubs are the year-round exceptions. Absolutely and Mandala Lounge were reported closed in October 2026. Levels Carvoeiro and Carvoeiro's Cocktail Garden maintain reduced winter hours but are worth checking directly before a visit.

The golden rule: city-centre bars (Lagos old town, Faro old town, Albufeira old town, Tavira square) survive winter better than beach-facing and cliff-top bars. Plan around towns rather than specific venues when visiting October–April.

## The case for the Algarve in winter

December to February in the Algarve: 15–18°C daytime, rarely below 10°C at night, uncrowded beaches, half the accommodation prices, and the bar experience you actually came for — local people, no queue, a bartender who has time to talk about what's in your glass. The weather is not a problem. The atmosphere is better.

## FAQ

Are Algarve bars open in winter?

Many are — particularly city-centre bars in Lagos, Faro, Albufeira and Tavira. Beach and cliff-top bars are the ones most likely to close or cut back significantly.

Which town has the best winter bar scene?

Lagos and Faro are the most consistent. Tavira is the best overall winter destination — quieter, more authentic, and the bars that stay open are the good ones.

What is the Algarve like in winter?

Mild (12–18°C), quiet, green and significantly cheaper. Many residents prefer it to summer. The bars that survive winter tend to be the ones locals actually use.

Do bars reduce hours in winter?

Almost all do. Even year-round bars typically open fewer nights or shorter hours from November to March. Always check directly before visiting.
