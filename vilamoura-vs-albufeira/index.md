## The case for Vilamoura

Vilamoura is the Algarve's most polished night out. [The Argo](https://www.artonbar.com/vilamoura/the-argo/) at the Tivoli Marina is the only bar in the region winning cocktail awards at the Iberian level — the best cocktail menu between Lisbon and Seville, credibly. Around it: the marina boardwalk, Baracuda (the oldest bar on the boardwalk), restaurants that stay lively late, and the casino when the night refuses to end.

The catch is cost and a certain sameness — it's a purpose-built resort, and it feels like one. For local character at local prices, neighbouring [Quarteira](https://www.artonbar.com/quarteira/) is a 35-minute seafront walk away and is where the Portuguese actually drink.

## The case for Albufeira

Albufeira delivers scale that Vilamoura simply doesn't attempt: the Strip is a full street of bars and clubs, open absurdly late, priced for groups drinking in rounds. If your night is measured in venues visited, nothing else in the Algarve competes.

And for one evening of actual quality, [Sal Rosa](https://www.artonbar.com/albufeira/sal-rosa/) on the old-town cliff edge holds its own against anything in Vilamoura — book the front tables at sunset.

## The verdict

**Choose Vilamoura** for polish: a couples trip, a golf trip, one exceptional cocktail bar, and a casino nightcap.

**Choose Albufeira** for the party: big group, late nights, maximum bars per euro.

**Secret third option:** stay in [Quarteira](https://www.artonbar.com/quarteira/) — local prices, promenade sunsets — and dip into Vilamoura's marina when you want the polish. That combination beats both for value.

## FAQ

Is Vilamoura or Albufeira better for nightlife?

Different nights. Vilamoura: marina polish, award-winning cocktails, casino. Albufeira: the Algarve's biggest party street. Sophisticated evening → Vilamoura. Group blowout → Albufeira.

Is Vilamoura more expensive?

Yes — €15–20 cocktails at the marina. The budget hack is staying in Quarteira next door at half the price.

Which is better for couples?

Vilamoura — The Argo at the marina. In Albufeira, go to Sal Rosa and skip the Strip.

Which is better in winter?

Vilamoura — The Argo and the casino run year-round while the Strip mostly closes.
