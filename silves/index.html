Silves & Monchique · Inland Algarve

Bars in Silves & Monchique: Castle Terraces, River Tapas & Medronho (2026)

Silves and Monchique are the inland Algarve: a red sandstone castle above an orange-grove river in one, a mountain village of chestnut trees and firewater in the other. Neither is a nightlife town. Both have a handful of bars worth the drive — a terrace under the castle at golden hour, a riverside tapas bar with craft beer, a 12th-century stone room in Caldas de Monchique that serves the local medronho properly.

Silves: drink where the view is

Silves's bars are about position. Café Inglês at the top of the castle steps has the best terrace in town — the Arade below, the fortress walls behind — and does a cold beer or a glass of regional white at golden hour better than any dinner it serves. Just below the fortress, Recanto dos Mouros pours regional wines by the glass on a terrace that looks up at the sandstone. Down by the Roman bridge, O Cais is the proper bar: tapas, craft beers, Portuguese wines and live music evenings.

The old streets between castle and river have a few more café-bars and tascas. None are destinations; all are cheap. Silves closes early — plan for a late-afternoon arrival, castle, drink, dinner, and the drive back to the coast.

Monchique: medronho and mountain air

Monchique is where medronho comes from — the arbutus-berry spirit distilled in the hills, 40-50% and tasting of it. Every café-bar in the village pours it; order it as a short after dinner, or as melosa (with honey) if you're new to it. Nightlife in Monchique means exactly this: a post-dinner medronho on a terrace at altitude, the coast's lights far below.

Down the hill in Caldas de Monchique, the thermal-spa village, the Alkaline Bar at the Pure Monchique Hotel is the one bar with real atmosphere: 12th-century stone walls, an old bread oven used as a table, a fireplace that's lit when the cold comes in, and Caldas bread with chouriço from the wood oven. Go on a winter afternoon.

Combine them

The classic inland day: Silves castle late morning, lunch by the river, drive up through Caldas to Fóia (the Algarve's highest point) for the view, medronho in Monchique village, back down to the coast by dark. Wine tastings at the Silves-area quintas slot in nicely. Everything's a 30–40 minute drive from Portimão, Lagoa or Carvoeiro.

FAQ — Silves & Monchique bars & nightlife

Is there nightlife in Silves?
Not really — bars close by midnight, earlier off-season. Silves is an afternoon-into-evening town with great terraces, not a night out.
What is medronho?
A spirit distilled from arbutus (strawberry tree) berries in the Monchique hills, usually 40-50% ABV. Drunk as a short after dinner; 'melosa' is the honey-sweetened version.
Where's the best drink with a castle view?
Café Inglês at the castle steps in Silves, or the terrace at Recanto dos Mouros just below the walls.
Can I do Silves and Monchique in one day?
Easily — they're 25 minutes apart by road, and both are 30–40 minutes from Portimão or Carvoeiro.
Are these bars open in winter?
Yes. Silves and Monchique are year-round towns; the Alkaline Bar in Caldas is arguably best in winter with the fire lit.

Things to do in Silves & Monchique

Silves and Monchique wine-tasting day trips, the solar boat up the Arade, and the cork factory tour.
